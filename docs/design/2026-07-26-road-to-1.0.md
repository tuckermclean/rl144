# Road to 1.0 — the plan of record (2026-07-26)

Converged human + agents, 2026-07-26. Division of labor that produced it:
the human holds the soul of the game, the agents hold the critical path, and
the tiebreaker is the human-locked story canon (`docs/story/STORY-COMPILE-v1.md`).
This doc exists because three plans in a row pronounced on §9-F scope from
memory instead of from the doc; the source for every batch below is the doc,
not this file. This file is the ratchet, not the authority.

## The honest diagnosis

Fifteen batches preferred goldenable work (mechanics, instruments, invariants)
and deferred the un-goldenable (writing, legibility, closure, feel) — the
incentive gradient of agent-driven development. The gap between here and a
playable game is almost entirely the deferred half. The game is mechanically
winnable today (round-trip to root depth-1 `<`), but the win is in the wrong
place and the descent is missing its cast.

## The revised shape (order is load-bearing)

**cast → ending-as-exam → polish/legibility/ship.**

The structural fact that fixes the order: **the minigames ARE the ending.**
Story §9-F lesson 2 (the coat's *answer the second voice*) and lesson 3 (the
mimic's *polite no / hold still and endure*) are, per the doc, "reused at the
mantel." §9-F and §9-I are one curriculum in two rooms — you teach on the
descent, the mantel is the exam. So the cast is built first; the ending mostly
*assembles* the framework the cast proves.

## Terminal-mechanics manifest (the freeze-as-ratchet)

The freeze is not a date — it's this list. The cast brief opens with the
**complete enumeration** of the mechanics 1.0 will contain; anything not on it
is post-1.0 by prior agreement, and the brief itself enforces the ratchet so
the cast batches can't creep themselves. Known terminal mechanics (to be
finalized and frozen at the head of the cast brief):

- the **talk-minigame framework** (offer/decline loops; answer-a-specific-voice) —
  the shared engine the mantel exam (§9-I) reuses;
- the **mimic's disguise/ambush** (chest-with-teeth; courtship-as-hunting; strikes
  on approach — NOT the passive/bump substrate);
- **sword custody / disarm** (§9-F D3) — see sign-off item 4 for the model decision;
- the **coat give-transform** (second coat → one tall monster becomes two short) —
  the first mid-run entity spawn in the codebase; MUST be deterministic/replay-safe
  (derive from state or a named per-run channel; no fresh entropy, no wall-clock);
- **the mantel exam + Judge** (§9-I) — win relocation + derived-state read; assembles
  from the framework above, invents no new interaction mode;
- whatever **hashed lesson-state** the mantel exam needs to remember the descent's
  lessons (enumerate at brief time; joins `state_hash` + a `SAVE_VERSION` bump).

Post-1.0, by prior agreement: ghost playback, Dest::World enrichment, donkey
rung 3 (the LOVE→descend→ogre-becalm reveal — the one cut that touches the
ending's emotional payoff; worth a human confirm before it's locked out),
audio, sprites, wasm/mobile, net relay.

## The NPC-vault worldgen MAJOR — sign-off package

The cast lives on the **root procedural dungeon** descent spine at fixed depths
(§9-F D2–D5), THE STAGE is the pickup room on the deepest floor, and the mimic
re-encounters you on the climb — so this is DECISION.md item 4's NPC-vault
MAJOR (fixed NPC rooms stamped via the vault channel), NOT authored-floors
(those are off-spine portal side-trips). The brief carries all of:

1. **It is band-moving, not just golden-moving.** The mimic's ambush strikes the
   *bots* pathing the spine, and guaranteed vaults change the geometry every
   policy walks — all four sim bands AND the ratified relational flip gate move.
   The brief must state the flip is EXPECTED to survive and MEASURE it.
   **Cascade:** the *polite no* and *answer-the-second-voice* are diplomacy
   interactions — the tactical-pacifist bot MUST learn them, or the post-cast
   diplomat number craters for an instrument reason (as silent-give-ground did
   to the goblin pre-batch-15). Teaching the bot the minigames is priced INSIDE
   the cast batch.
2. **Pickup relocates to THE STAGE**, replacing deepest-BFS objective placement →
   the solver's walk-budget distribution shifts → **`START_LIGHT` must be
   re-derived** from the new worst case via the probe machinery, not assumed.
3. **Guaranteed placement is a new invariant class.** THE STAGE on D5 and the
   cast at their fixed depths must exist on EVERY seed — a new solver-side
   assertion plus golden coverage, and (fixture-blind-spot lesson) the dump
   fixtures must VISIBLY contain the new rooms.
4. **Sword custody collides with the item model — RULED: stat-flag `disarm`,
   three riders (human pre-ratified 2026-07-26).** The sword is walk-over
   `Consume` (+2 ATK, gone) — you can't entrust what you don't hold. `Hold`
   (the general answer) would reprice the violent route's economy as a side
   effect of one scene — the exact coupling the bands would spend a batch
   untangling — so it's rejected in favor of a surgical transient flag. But the
   surgical version must keep the canon's *meaning*, which is TRUST (you hand
   your weapon to the hungry furniture), so the flag carries three riders:
   - **(a) choice-routed, never imposed.** The disarm goes through the same
     offer/decline framework the mimic's polite-no uses — the mimic offers to
     hold your sword, you *accept*. A flag set AT you is theft; a flag you set
     by choosing is the mercy thesis in miniature (disarming yourself before the
     thing that might eat you), and it's free because the offer/decline loop is
     being built in this batch anyway.
   - **(b) hashed.** Effective ATK differs while disarmed → run-defining state,
     not presentation → joins `state_hash` + the `SAVE_VERSION` bump.
   - **(c) lifecycle specified in the brief, not discovered.** What restores ATK
     (presumably the mimic's climb re-encounter) AND the edges — the mimic dies
     while holding your sword, you portal out mid-custody, you retry mid-custody
     — are enumerated up front. An unspecified transient flag's edges are exactly
     where next month's playtest bug lives.
5. **The coat split-spawn** is new engine surface (first mid-run spawn) — on the
   manifest with the determinism constraint above.

## Gates specific to this stretch

- **Human plays the mimic** between cast batch 1 (the mimic — the
  framework-proving character) and cast batch 2 + the ending brief. The exam is
  built out of the mimic's lesson; the lesson must be play-tested before the exam
  is assembled. Every structural bug the instruments couldn't see was found by
  playing.

  net that lets the cast move the bands without silently inverting the thesis.

## Count
