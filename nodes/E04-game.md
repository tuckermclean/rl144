**Owns:** `src/game.rs`, the engine core.

## Purpose
`src/game.rs` is the engine core: the grid constants (`COLS` 80, `ROWS` 30, `MAP_H` 25), the world state
(`Tile`, `Monster`, `Item`, `LevelState`, `WorldId`, `Dest`, `WorldState`, `Game`), the grid helpers `idx`
and `in_map`, the solver's `bfs_dist`, and all of `Game`'s behaviour:
- **Construction and worldgen**: `Game::new(seed)` (the frozen root-dungeon D1 constructor used by every
  headless surface) and `Game::new_overworld(seed)` (the interactive and replay front door), both over
  `Game::base`; `gen_level` (rooms, corridors, spawns, the optional vault roll, `place_required_vault_room`'s
  exhaustive scan for the guaranteed cast vaults, `stamp_vault`), all on the per-depth `worldgen`, `spawns`,
  `vault` channels.
- **Turns and light**: `apply_input` maps the input-byte vocabulary (0-3 move/bump, 4 wait or portal transit,
  7-10 talk, 11-14 give, 15 use-top, 16 put-down, `17+kind` use-by-kind, `17+len(items)+kind` set-down-by-kind;
  any other byte is ignored; 5 and 6 are handled by `save::replay`); `spend_turn` burns the torch (1 a turn, 2
  carrying the objective, plus the violence tax per bump-attack; none in the overworld), then `monsters_act`
  and `compute_fov` (per-source light radii in `lit_r`, the McGuffin's mood-driven shine via
  `mcguffin_light`); the lose check (darkness unless lit by the McGuffin) runs before the win check.
- **Mercy and combat**: `receptivity` (the parley-roll probability, its one definition), talk and becalm,
  per-creature awe (`resolve_awe`, `monsters_act_and_resolve_awe`), ogre retaliation and punish hits, the
  polite-decline talk minigame, disguise and reveal, resting heal, the becalm dividend, kill and spare records
  feeding `mood`.
- **Items**: walk-over pickup (`Hold` or `Consume`), the LIFO `held` list with `recompute_atk`, give
  (`give_table`), use and use-by-kind, put-down and set-down-by-kind with the once-per-monster disarm regard,
  light caches capped per run.
- **The McGuffin's voice**: `carry_event` dispatch over `carried_lines` on the flavor channel only.
- **Worlds**: stair descent and ascent with `LevelState` persistence, portals (destination rolled once,
  walk-on describes, wait transits), authored floors keyed by `WorldId::Floor`, the three overworld screens
  (screen links, the hole into the root dungeon, the shut door), passive and bump-response monsters
  (`Fight`, `Yield`, `Shove`) and the calm donkey's follow step; sokoban pushes (chains of at most
  `MAX_PUSH_CHAIN` = 2, pits fill, goals lock).

Every game fact comes from `crate::games::GAME`; monster and item kinds are plain indices into its tables.

## Tests
All inline in `src/main.rs` (`mod tests`), run with
`PATH="$HOME/.cargo/bin:$PATH" RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN=1.75 cargo test --quiet <name>`; the criteria below name them.
The whole-engine checks are the `Makefile` targets of the gate: `goldens` (`tests/golden/seed_*.txt`),
`frames` (`tests/golden/frame_seed_*.bin`), `xhash` (`tests/fixtures/ref.sav` through both backends),
`solve` (`tests/solver-band.json`), `sim` (`tests/sim-band.json`, `tests/pacifist-band.json`,
`tests/tactical-band.json`, `tests/tactical-pacifist-band.json`) and `flip`.

## Risks
- Any change to worldgen (channel tags, draw order or count on `worldgen`, `spawns`, `vault`, room, corridor or
  placement logic) diffs the goldens: a MAJOR change that needs the owner's sign-off before it is built, then
  the re-baseline flow of `CLAUDE.md` (goldens, `--solve 10000`, `tests/solver-band.json`, `START_LIGHT`).
- A new input byte or a change to the shape of hashed state needs a `SAVE_VERSION` bump in `src/save.rs`;
  which `Game` fields `state_hash` covers is listed there, not here (the tests that pin it, such as
  `blocks_are_hashed` and `held_is_hashed`, belong with that file).
- A new move on a route needs the bot that models the route taught in the same change (instrument parity), or
  the band measurement is an artifact.
- The file is about 4,500 lines; `game.rs` and `src/headless.rs` refer to each other (`world_hash`), and it
  imports `render::Facing`. Any split of the file is a design decision for the owner (`CLAUDE.md` asks to
  keep the module count).
- Every test is in `src/main.rs`, which only the implementer may write until the tests move to
  `src/tests/*.rs` (`SPEC.md`'s open decisions).
