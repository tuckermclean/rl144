**Owns:** `tests/golden/`, the frozen map dumps and terminal frames.

## Purpose
`tests/golden/` holds the outputs that freeze worldgen and the first frame. Nothing here is code; each file is
the committed output of a headless mode and is compared byte for byte.
- **Map goldens** `seed_1.txt`, `seed_2.txt`, `seed_3.txt`, `seed_42.txt` and `seed_1337.txt`: the
  `--dump --seed N` output (`headless::dump`) of seeds 1, 2, 3, 42 and 1337, 136 lines each: a `seed=N` line,
  then for each of the five depths of `Game::new(N)` a `-- depth d : <theme label> --` header, 25 map rows of
  80 characters (the legend at `headless::level_dump`) and a `monsters=N items=M` line. They are worldgen's
  public API (`CLAUDE.md`: a change to a map layout is a MAJOR version event). Last regenerated by the mimic
  batch's NPC-vault MAJOR (commit 4ea78bf).
- **Frame goldens** `frame_seed_1.bin`, `frame_seed_42.bin` and `frame_seed_1_ascii.bin`: the terminal
  build's `--render-frame --seed N` output for seeds 1 and 42, and with `--ascii` for seed 1: the full-redraw
  ANSI byte stream of the Play screen of a fresh `Game::new(N)` (P01's grid, P03's encoding). Last
  regenerated in commit 7e329fc.

## Tests
`golden_dumps` in `src/main.rs` compiles the map goldens in (`include_str!`) and compares each with
`dump(N)`: run it with `PATH="$HOME/.cargo/bin:$PATH" RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN=1.75 cargo test --quiet golden_dumps`.
`make goldens` compares them with the release binary's `--dump --seed N`, and `make frames` compares the frame
goldens with the terminal build's `--render-frame` (both targets of `Makefile`, which print
`golden mismatch for seed N` and `frame golden mismatch for seed N`).

## Gate
- **M02-AC-1** — Each `seed_N.txt` is exactly what `dump(N)` returns and what `--dump --seed N` prints, for N in 1, 2, 3, 42 and 1337. *Verify:* `golden_dumps` in `src/main.rs`; `make goldens` (target of `Makefile`).
- **M02-AC-2** — `frame_seed_1.bin` and `frame_seed_42.bin` are exactly what the terminal build's `--render-frame --seed N` prints for seeds 1 and 42, and `frame_seed_1_ascii.bin` what `--render-frame --seed 1 --ascii` prints. *Verify:* `make frames` (target of `Makefile`).
- **M02-AC-3** — Each map golden has the shape described above: a `seed=N` line and five depth sections of a header, 25 rows of 80 characters and a `monsters=N items=M` line. *Verify:* unpinned (the tests compare the files with the dump, not with a shape; a change to the dump format and its goldens together passes them).

## Risks
- These files are frozen by the criteria of E01, E02, E04, C01, S02, P01 and P03 and by this node:
  `SPEC.md`'s open decisions allow a map golden to change only with the owner's MAJOR sign-off (or, for a
  dump-format-only change, the owner's approval, the commit saying so), and a frame golden only with the
  owner's approval.
- `golden_dumps` reads the map goldens at compile time, so deleting or renaming one breaks the whole test
  build, not one test; `make goldens` and `golden_dumps` must agree, and both read the same five seeds
  (`GOLDEN_SEEDS` in the `Makefile`, the list in the test).
- The frame goldens freeze only turn 0 of the Play screen at full torch; the Title and End screens and the
  minifb backend's pixels have no golden.
- Regenerating them is by hand: `--dump --seed N > tests/golden/seed_N.txt` and the terminal build's
  `--render-frame` redirected to the `.bin` files; no target writes them.
